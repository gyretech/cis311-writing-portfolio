About me

Built on experience.

I have worked in professional software development since 2010. My background also includes systems administration and desktop support. That experience has taught me to look at a problem from both sides: the person building the solution and the person who has to use it.

In enterprise IT, I work with developers, business users, and leadership. Whether I am explaining a design decision or documenting a process, I want the reader to understand what matters and why.

This portfolio brings that perspective to my work in CIS311: Technical Writing in CIS at Post University.

My approach

Give the reader a clear path.

I approach writing much as I approach software: understand the user, organize the information, and remove assumptions that get in the way.

The samples demonstrate source-based research, a measurable proposal, step-by-step instructions, data interpretation, and respectful business communication. References remain with the research and proposal so readers can trace the supporting evidence.
