World Orange Production, 2017–2021

An analysis that pairs exact figures with a concise explanation of production trends.

Table 1 and Figure 1 summarize orange production, including tangerines, for eight major producing countries and regions from 2017 through 2021. The table gives the exact production values, while the chart makes the five-year trends easier to compare. Brazil shows the largest increase in total output, rising from 12,053 to 18,998 thousand metric tons, an increase of about 58%. India and the European Union also increase overall, while Egypt records the largest percentage growth at about 63%, despite remaining a smaller producer by total volume. The United States shows the clearest decline, falling from 8,562 to 4,500 thousand metric tons, or about 47%, after a temporary increase in 2019. China fluctuates substantially across the period, Mexico declines overall, and Spain remains relatively stable. By 2021, Brazil is clearly the largest producer shown, followed by India, suggesting that the strongest production growth in this group shifted toward Brazil and several other non-U.S. producers during the five-year period.
