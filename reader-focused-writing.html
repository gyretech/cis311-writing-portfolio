Audience & tone · Writing sample 05

Writing With the Reader in Mind

Five business-writing revisions that replace blame and vague requests with clear next steps.

1. Original: Something is obviously wrong in your head office. They have once more sent me the wrong model number. Can they ever get things straight?

Revised: Please verify the requested model number and send the correct model. Your attention to the order details will help ensure the replacement is processed accurately.

2. Original: My instructor wants me to do a term paper on safety regulations at a small plant. Since you are the manager of a small plant, send me all the information I need at once. My grade depends heavily on all this.

Revised: Your experience managing a small plant would provide valuable insight for a term paper on workplace safety regulations. Could you please share any information or resources about the safety practices and regulations used at your plant when convenient?

3. Original: It is apparent that you are in business to rip off the public.

Revised: Could you please review the charges and explain how they were determined? Your help in clarifying the costs would be appreciated and may help resolve any concerns.

4. Original: I was wondering if you could possibly see your way into sending me the local chapter president’s name and address—if you have the time, that is.

Revised: Could you please send the name and address of the local chapter president when convenient? Thank you for your assistance.

5. Original: I have waited for my confirmation for two weeks now. Do you expect me to wait forever, or can I get some action?

Revised: Could you please check the status of the confirmation and provide an update on when it can be expected? An update would be helpful since two weeks have passed.
